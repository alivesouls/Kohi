Our Philosophy
One
Origin is everything

Every bean carries the story of its land. We trace each lot to its specific farm, altitude, and harvest — because provenance is the first act of quality.

Two
Roasted with restraint

We roast light to medium — never dark. The goal is to reveal, not to hide. A bean roasted too dark loses the very character that made it worth sourcing.

Three
Brewed with precision

Temperature, grind, ratio, time — these are not variables to approximate. They are the variables we have mastered, cup by cup, day by day, for you.

Why us?
"

"Every cup we serve is a letter written to the land it came from."

Kōhī was born from a belief that great coffee is an act of respect — for the farmer, the bean, and the person drinking it. We work directly with small producers in Ethiopia and Colombia, paying above fair-trade prices and visiting every farm we source from.
